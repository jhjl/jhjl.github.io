# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **jhjl** ("the app", "we", "us") handles
information when you sign in with your Google Account and use the app. The app
is operated by jhjl and is available at <https://jhjl.github.io>.

## Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests your permission to access the
following Google user data:

| Data | Why it is needed |
| --- | --- |
| Your basic Google profile — name, email address and profile picture | To identify you and sign you in |
| Google Drive files that you create with, or explicitly open in, the app | To provide the app's core file functionality |

The app only accesses Drive files you select or that it creates. It does not
scan, index or read the rest of your Drive.

We do not ask for, and do not access, your password, contacts, calendar, Gmail,
location, or payment information.

## How the information is used

The information is used solely to:

- authenticate you and show you your own account;
- let you view and edit the Drive files you chose to work with.

We do **not** use your Google user data for advertising, profiling, marketing,
credit assessment, or to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence
models. We do **not** sell your data.

## Storage and retention

The app does not operate a server that stores your Google user data. Your
profile information and Drive file contents are processed on your own device
while you use the app and are not uploaded to us.

OAuth access tokens issued by Google are kept only on your device, in the app's
local storage, so that you do not have to sign in every time. They are never
sent to a third party. Tokens remain until you sign out or revoke access.

## Sharing

We do not share, transfer or disclose your Google user data to any third party,
except:

- where you explicitly ask the app to share a file;
- where we are required to do so by law.

## Your choices and control

You stay in control of your Google data at all times:

- **Revoke access:** visit
  <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>, select the app and choose
  *Remove access*. The app will immediately lose the ability to access your
  Google Account and Drive.
- **Sign out** in the app to delete the locally stored tokens from your device.
- **Delete your data:** because we do not store your data, revoking access and
  signing out removes everything the app holds. Files in your Google Drive
  remain yours and can be deleted in Drive at any time.

## Security

Access tokens are stored locally and transmitted only to Google's own API
endpoints over HTTPS. We do not maintain a database of user data that could be
breached.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

jhjl's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google
APIs will adhere to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from them.
